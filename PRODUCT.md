# Product

> **One portal.** This repo is the DI design portal: one site with the DI look, holding DI material and Giga material (Giga logos, colours and video guidelines). The older text below was written for the Giga brand site and is kept for its design rules; read "Giga" there as the Giga material inside this portal.

## Register

product

> The platform is a utility, so operational surfaces (email signature builder, map generator, downloads, settings) use the **product** register. Public and landing surfaces, above all the **home page**, intentionally use the **brand** register: expressive, photography-led, made to communicate. Pick the register from the surface in focus; the home and any marketing/overview page are brand, everything operational is product. Both share the one visual system in DESIGN.md.

## Users

Brand and communications teams at Digital Inclusion, UNICEF, ITU, and authorized partner organizations. They are not designers; they need the tool to make the formatting decisions for them so they can focus on communicating. The same surfaces double as the public face of the Giga design system, so visitors form an impression of the brand here too.

## Product Purpose

Self-serve brand asset platform for the Digital Inclusion design system. Partners find and download correct, on-brand assets (logos, colors, typography, illustrations, media, maps, email signatures, backgrounds, social templates) plus the AI skills the Digital Inclusion team builds with, without involving the design team. Success means zero off-brand assets in the wild. The home page sells that promise; the inner tools deliver it.

## Brand Personality

Institutional precision with quiet authority, warmed by mission. The tool embodies the Giga brand rather than inventing one of its own: it stays out of the way and delivers. On brand-register surfaces it gains one note of warmth, real photography of connected classrooms and the people Giga serves, always disciplined by Giga Blue and generous white space. Confident, never loud.

## Anti-references

- **SaaS hype.** No gradient text, no glowing buttons, no glassmorphism-by-default, no "magical AI" marketing language.
- **Clutter, "too much".** No decorative illustration spam, no second accent color, no busy tiles. Balance, elegance, and cleanliness win; when in doubt, remove.
- **Black slabs.** No solid black or Grey-950 backgrounds anywhere except Giga Maps. Dark means a photograph or a neutral scrim over one, never a flat black panel.
- **Off-brand color.** Giga Blue is the only voice color and the only colored background; no yellow or lime accents borrowed from reference layouts.

## Design Principles

1. The brand is the product. Every surface should look like it was made to the standard it enforces.
2. Utility over decoration on tool surfaces; communication over decoration on brand surfaces. Decoration for its own sake, never.
3. Consistency over cleverness. Novel patterns break partner trust; established ones build it. One visual system (DESIGN.md) across every page.
4. White space is not emptiness. Generous margins signal quality.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA minimum. No reliance on color alone for meaning. All motion honors `prefers-reduced-motion`. Keep legible contrast for white type over photography (neutral scrim plus a focused gradient where text sits).
